What this is

The temperature and supply voltages come from the FPGA's built-in XADC monitor, read over JTAG by the Raspberry Pi CM4 sitting next to it.

The ring oscillators are five free-running loops of logic gates placed in the corners and middle of the die. How fast they run depends on the silicon, the core voltage and the temperature, so they double as on-chip sensors.

Built with a fully open-source FPGA toolchain (yosys, nextpnr-xilinx, Project X-Ray).

Read-only. Updates every 5 s; history is stored every 10 s.
