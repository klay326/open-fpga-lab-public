How it's wired

A Raspberry Pi CM4 sits next to the FPGA. It reads the FPGA over two UARTs (the analog lab and the TRNG) and reads the XADC sensor over JTAG. It also runs this page, the safety limits and the self-calibration of the ring oscillators against the XADC.

Safety: the heater cuts out at 72 °C and every session ends after 10 minutes. The FPGA also turns the heater off by itself if the Pi goes quiet, and its own over-temperature alarm is armed. Everything is built with open-source tools (yosys, nextpnr-xilinx, Project X-Ray).

Updates every second. History is sampled every 10 s and kept for 30 days.
